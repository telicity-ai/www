Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Telicity (텔리시티) handles the personal information of people who visit telicity.ai, sign up for news there, or write to hello@telicity.ai as this policy describes. As Article 30 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) requires, it sets out what we collect and why, how long we keep it, and when and how we delete it. We process only the minimum each purpose needs.

This is an English translation of the Korean policy at telicity.ai/ko/privacy/. If the two differ, the Korean version applies.

In short

  • When you sign up for news, the only thing you type is your email address. We keep a record of your consent with it.
  • We use that address only to send Telicity launch and product news, which counts as advertising. For now we are only collecting sign-ups; we haven’t sent any email yet.
  • If you unsubscribe at telicity.ai/unsubscribe/, we delete your address and consent record from our list right away.
  • We don’t use cookies or analytics tools.

1. Why we process personal information

We process personal information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, we will tell you first and ask for your consent again where needed.

  1. To email you Telicity launch and product news (advertising emails).
  2. To check every two years, as Korean law requires, whether you want to keep receiving them.
  3. To record and prove your sign-up and consent.
  4. To prevent abuse such as automated sign-ups.
  5. To serve the website and keep it secure.
  6. To answer questions and requests to exercise your rights.

2. What we process, and the legal basis

With your consent

  • Your email address
  • A record of your consent: the date and time, the version of the notice you agreed to, the language you signed up in (Korean or English), and the address of the page you signed up on

The legal basis is PIPA Article 15(1)(1), your consent. We ask separately for your consent to receive advertising, under Article 50(1) of Korea’s Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection (the Network Act). We keep every version of the consent notice, so we can show what you agreed to.

Without your consent

PIPA Article 22(3) asks us to list these separately from what we process with consent.

  • IP hash: a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256) of the IP address you connect from. We never store the IP address itself. We use the hash only to count sign-up and unsubscribe requests from the same network over a short time and stop automated requests. It is deleted automatically within 1 hour. Basis: PIPA Article 15(1)(6), our legitimate interest.
  • Website access logs: the IP address, time, requested page address, and browser information that your browser sends when you open a page, sign up, or unsubscribe. Our host, Vercel, processes them automatically to serve the site and keep it secure. The email address you sign up with is not written to these logs. How long Vercel keeps them is set by Vercel’s privacy policy (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), which gives no fixed period. We don’t keep copies. Basis: PIPA Article 15(1)(6).
  • Email you send to hello@telicity.ai: your email address, your name if you include it, and what you write. Used to answer you. Kept for 1 year after we reply. Basis: PIPA Article 15(1)(2), a legal duty, for requests to exercise your rights; PIPA Article 15(1)(6) for other questions.

3. How long we keep it

  • We keep your email address and consent record until you withdraw your consent, then delete them without delay.
  • Every two years from the day you agreed, we will remind you that you agreed, and on what date, and ask whether you want to keep receiving news. If you don’t confirm by the date we give, we delete your data. If we can’t send that reminder, we delete your data before the second anniversary of your consent.
  • If Telicity decides to stop sending news, or closes, we delete everything.
  • The IP hash, access logs, and emails to hello@telicity.ai are kept for the periods in section 2.
  • No other law requires us to keep any of this information.

4. How we delete it

  • Once there is a reason to delete data, we delete it without delay, and within 5 days at the latest. If you unsubscribe at telicity.ai/unsubscribe/, it is deleted right then.
  • Electronic data is deleted so it cannot be recovered. Exported copies and copies in our mailbox are deleted too.
  • Upstash, which stores sign-ups for us, backs up the database regularly and keeps the backups in the eastern United States, where the database is. Deleted email addresses, consent records, and IP hashes can stay in these backups until Upstash’s backup deletion schedule removes them. Upstash says it may keep backup copies for up to 4 weeks after a database itself is deleted. We don’t make backups of our own, and we never restore deleted data from a backup.
  • Email we delete from our Gmail mailbox is fully removed from Google’s servers through Google’s deletion process, which generally takes about 2 months. It may remain in Google’s encrypted backups for up to 6 months.
  • We log the date, reason, number of records, and method of every deletion, and our privacy officer checks it. The log never includes the deleted email addresses.

5. Sharing with third parties

We don’t give your personal information to third parties, except where PIPA Articles 17 and 18 allow it, for example when a law specifically requires it.

6. Service providers who process data for us

  • Vercel Inc.: website hosting, processing sign-ups and unsubscribes (running the server function), keeping access logs
  • Upstash, Inc.: storing sign-up data (email address, consent record, IP hash)
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: forwarding email sent to hello@telicity.ai
  • Google LLC (Gmail): storing email received at hello@telicity.ai

If a provider or its task changes, we will update this policy. Before we start sending news, we will add our email delivery provider here and tell you about it in the first email.

How to refuse, and what happens

To refuse the transfer, don’t sign up, or unsubscribe if you already have. You then won’t receive the news. We take questions by email only, so if you refuse the transfer of email, you can’t write to us. You can still unsubscribe at telicity.ai/unsubscribe/ without sending an email. The transfer of website access logs can be avoided only by not using the website.

8. How we keep it safe

  • All traffic between your browser and our server, and between our server and the database, is encrypted (TLS).
  • The database access key is kept only in server environment variables, never in a web page.
  • Our Vercel, Upstash, GitHub, and email admin accounts use two-step verification.
  • We never store raw IP addresses, and our server logs never contain email addresses.
  • Only the people who need to handle personal information can access it.
  • If we ever need to export the list, we keep the export encrypted and delete it after use.
  • We keep a record of every access, export, and deletion of personal information for at least 1 year.

9. Cookies and automatic collection

telicity.ai doesn’t use cookies. We don’t use analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking tools. Fonts and images are all served from telicity.ai itself, so opening a page sends no requests to outside font services or trackers.

10. Your rights and how to use them

  • You can ask at any time to see, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information, and you can withdraw your consent.
  • To withdraw consent and delete your data, enter your email address at telicity.ai/unsubscribe/. It takes effect right away. You don’t need to log in or give anything else. You can also ask at hello@telicity.ai.
  • Send any other request to hello@telicity.ai. We will act on it within 10 days of receiving it and tell you the result.
  • To check that a request comes from you, we may reply to the address you signed up with.
  • A legal guardian or someone you authorize can also make requests. Please include a letter of authorization.
  • If the law doesn’t let us do what you ask, we will tell you why and how to object. If you disagree, write to hello@telicity.ai and we will review it again and tell you the result.
  • When you consent to, refuse, or withdraw consent to advertising emails, we tell you the sender, what you did and on what date, and the result within 14 days. If you do it on our website, we show this on the screen right away.
  • Please don’t sign up with someone else’s email address. Doing so can infringe their rights.

11. Advertising emails

  • We send them only to people who have agreed.
  • Each subject line starts with (광고), the Korean label for advertising. Each email gives the sender’s name, email address, phone number, and postal address, and explains how to unsubscribe, in Korean and English.
  • Unsubscribing needs no login and costs nothing. Your email address is enough, and it takes effect right away.
  • Every two years from the day you agreed, we tell you that you agreed, on what date, and how to keep or withdraw your consent.
  • We aren’t sending any email yet. We will update this policy before we start.

12. Children under 14

We don’t accept sign-ups from children under 14. The sign-up form asks you to confirm that you are 14 or older. If we learn that we hold a child’s information, we delete it without delay.
