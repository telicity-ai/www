# Telicity

> Telicity (telicity.ai) is a company building the operating system and infrastructure for AI, starting with a personal AI assistant and twin that analyzes a person’s intent, turns it into a clear goal, and completes it.

**Telicity is building a personal AI assistant that turns what you mean into a clear goal, then sees it through.**

Over time it becomes your twin, learning how you decide. It’s our first step toward the operating system and infrastructure for AI.

In linguistics, telicity means having an endpoint: not “run,” but “run a mile.” We’d rather ask one good question than ten, and we know when to stop.

Some Sunday evening, you’ll be on your weekly call with Mom, telling her the dates for the trip you two kept calling “someday.” Neither the call nor the trip will have become one more thing to remember.

That’s the kind of week we’re building toward, when the back of your mind goes quiet.

We’ll write when there’s news. [Sign up for news](https://telicity.ai/#waitlist)

## Facts

- Name: Telicity. Korean name: 텔리시티. Also written as Telicity AI.
- Website: https://telicity.ai/ (English) and https://telicity.ai/ko/ (Korean)
- Mission: building the operating system and infrastructure for AI.
- First product: a personal AI assistant and twin that analyzes a person’s intent, turns it into a clear goal, and completes it.
- Status: in development.
- Name origin: telicity (n.), from Greek telos, “end, purpose”: in linguistics, the property of an action or event that has a built-in endpoint, as in “run a mile” versus “run.” The company takes its name from it because it is building software that carries a person’s intent through to a finished result.
