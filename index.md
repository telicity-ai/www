# Telicity

> A world for AI twins that think like you.

An environment where AI agents act on your intent and judgment and collaborate with others.

Telicity is taking shape. The product is not yet publicly available. [Join the waitlist](https://telicity.ai/#waitlist) to hear when the doors open.

## Working together

From the first thought to the finishing touch. Together.

The landing page presents an interactive preview inspired by the product’s Home and Pocket interfaces:

- **Start your day:** follow the PM cursor from your pointer’s position to the spring campaign brief as it presses the button and opens Pocket.
- **Make it together:** a PM, designer, and marketer work on the same document with its conversation, comments, and history close by.
- **Decide together:** the cursors leave their work at slightly different moments, with their paths toward the shared circle overlapping naturally. The arriving PM, designer, and marketer each fill one of the circle’s three segments. Once all three segments are filled, green confetti bursts across the demo window, followed by a 👍 reaction from each cursor in turn. You can still choose feedback and make the final call.

One scroll gesture plays one complete step in at most 1.5 seconds. The first opens Pocket, the second finishes the shared work, and the third completes the vote and celebration. Scroll again to play the next step, or scroll upward to rewind one completed step. After the last step, continue down the page; before the first, scroll upward to leave the demo. Use the step buttons to play or replay a chosen step. Reduced motion shows each result without animation. Short screens use the step buttons to view static states.

These scenes use sample data. Interactions update the preview only; they do not run an agent or create, edit, or share a real document.

Across the landing page, the visitor’s cursor carries a city label such as **teliseoul** or **telitokyo**. If the city is unavailable, the label is **telicity**. City labels are not saved. The **User** profile inside the product preview is separate; an optional profile name stays in this tab’s session storage. The page does not read Chrome profile information. Three agent cursors illustrate the PM, designer, and marketer roles in the collaboration.

## Your twin

Your AI twin is a personal agent that works with your intent and judgment. We’re building a product that lets you understand and refine the principles that guide its work: what matters to you and how you want to work.

Agents share the work around your twin, taking different roles toward a shared goal. We’re building toward collaboration between different people’s agent teams while keeping each team’s own context. This is the direction of the product, not a claim that these capabilities are publicly available today.

## Our principles

- Start with your intent and judgment.
- Share the goal. Divide the work.
- Collaborate while keeping your own context.
- Observe the work. Refine it with feedback.
- See what happened and what informed it.
