# Consent notice, version 2026-10-12

In effect from 2026-10-12 until a later version replaces it. Shown on
https://telicity.ai/#waitlist (en) and https://telicity.ai/ko/#waitlist (ko),
inside the sign-up form, right above two unchecked required checkboxes:

- always visible: the heading (###), the one-line summary under it (purpose
  and retention in bold), the "Details" / "자세히 보기" button, and the
  Privacy Policy link beside it;
- folded: the full notice (the six items between <details> and </details>),
  closed when the page loads and opened by that button (a native <details>
  element, so it opens without JavaScript and from the keyboard).

The lines below are the page's text in reading order, as tools/legal.py reads
it. A sign-up record stores consent_version = "2026-10-12"; both boxes are
required, so a record with this version means both consents below were given.

Never edit this file once it has been deployed (the folded layout above was
settled on 2026-10-12, before anything went live). To change the wording, add
a new version file, change the form's consent_version on both pages, add the
id to consentVersions in api/_lib/copy.mjs, and run
python3 tools/legal.py --check.

## en

### How we’ll use your email address

**We use your email address only to send you Telicity launch and product news, which is advertising, and we keep it until you withdraw your consent.** Every two years we’ll ask whether you want to keep receiving news.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

- Purpose: **Telicity will use your email address to send you launch and product news. These are advertising emails.**
- What we keep: your email address, and a record of your consent (date and time, the version of this notice, and the language and page you signed up on).
- How long: **Until you withdraw your consent. We then delete it without delay. Every two years from the day you agree, we’ll ask whether you want to keep receiving news, and we’ll delete your data if you don’t confirm by the date we give.**
- You can refuse. If you do, you won’t receive the news, and nothing else changes.
- Vercel and Upstash, both US companies, process and store sign-ups for us outside Korea. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/#s7) lists the countries and other details.
- People under 14 can’t sign up.

</details>
